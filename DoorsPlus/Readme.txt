DOORS+ for Ostranauts
A product of Cadaver Laboratories. MIT licence.

More doors.

Doors+ adds 17 new doors. They open. They close. They lock, unlock, take
power and signals, shut themselves behind you, and can be pried open when
they will not. Let me know if a door can't do something it needs to.

MORE GAME DOORS
We got the standard door, in more sizes of course. As well as the red and blast door. Smaller
doors for smaller problems. Larger doors for, more airflow? 


SLIDING DOORS
The same doors with one... door. Opening to whichever side you turn them to.
Standard, red and blast.


SPECIALIST DOORS
Standard, red and blast housings. The Folding Door is not a pressure door.
Please stop using it as one.


DROP DOORS
The slab sinks into the deck and the doorway becomes floor. A Drop Door is
the floor where it stands: install one and it takes the floor's place,
uninstall one and the floor goes with it...

QUARTERS DOORS
For the closet, the latrine and the cabin you share with someone you did
not choose. Hinged, opened by hand, a wall when shut.


INSTALL

Workshop: subscribe, then enable Doors+ in the game's Mods menu.
By hand: copy this folder to Ostranauts_Data/Mods/DoorsPlus and add
"DoorsPlus" to aLoadOrder in Ostranauts_Data/Mods/loading_order.json.

There is no plugin. There is nothing else to install. Maybe I'll add a plugin if people want custom sounds or animations.
